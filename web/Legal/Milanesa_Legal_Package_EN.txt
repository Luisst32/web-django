1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
They help us remember your preferences and ensure the Platform functions
correctly.

2. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRICTLY NECESSARY COOKIES (No consent required)
  - User session             : Keeps your session active while browsing.
  - Authentication token     : Verifies your identity securely.
  - CSRF protection          : Protects against cross-site request
                               forgery attacks.
  Without these cookies, the Platform will not function.

FUNCTIONAL COOKIES (Consent required)
  - Language preferences     : Remembers your preferred language.
  - Notification tokens      : Stored via Firebase for push notifications
                               (LocalStorage / IndexedDB).

ANALYTICS COOKIES (Consent required)
  - Google Analytics         : Measures traffic anonymously. Individual
                               users are not identified.

We do NOT use advertising or retargeting cookies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can control cookies in your browser settings:

  - Google Chrome    : Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  - Mozilla Firefox  : Preferences > Privacy > Cookies

You can also manage your cookie preferences directly on the Platform at:
Settings > Privacy > Cookie Management.

IMPORTANT: Disabling necessary cookies will prevent access to the Platform.
